02 / PW ACCOUNT

Use your PW session

Sign in on PW’s official website, then paste the Authorization value from your own session here.

We verify the session with PW and store it encrypted on your VPS. Keep this value private; it grants access to your account.

How do I get my Authorization value?
  1. Open PW’s official site and finish login there.
  2. Open your browser’s Developer Tools → Network. Reload PW and open a request to api.penpencil.co.
  3. Under Request Headers, copy the Authorization value. Choose a request from your logged-in account, not a public asset.
  4. Paste only that value above. Do not copy all headers or share the token in chat.

A token may expire or be tied to a device. If PW rejects it, nothing is saved.
